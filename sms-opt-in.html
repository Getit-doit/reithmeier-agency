Text Message (SMS) Opt-In & Call-to-Action

This page describes how consumers opt in to receive text messages from Reithmeier Agency LLC and the terms of the messaging program.

How to opt in

To opt in, visit our quote request page, enter your mobile phone number in the quote request form, review the consent disclosure shown with the form, and check the optional SMS consent checkbox, then submit the form. Checking the box is optional and is not required to receive a quote. You may also opt in by texting 940-323-4303. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

Consent disclosure shown at opt-in

Consent is granted by affirmatively checking the optional checkbox below at the point the mobile number is collected. The box is not pre-checked and is not required to submit the quote request:

Program terms

  • Program: Reithmeier Agency LLC account notifications, customer care, and marketing.
  • Message frequency varies.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Text STOP to any message to unsubscribe (opt out) at any time.
  • Text HELP for help, or contact 940-323-4303 or Info@reithmeieragency.com.
  • Mobile opt-in information and consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing purposes.
  • Message program privacy policy: Privacy Policy; full terms: SMS Terms & Conditions.

Sample messages

  • Reithmeier Agency LLC: Hi [Name], your insurance quote is ready. Reply or call 940-323-4303 to review. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.
  • Reithmeier Agency LLC: Reminder—your policy payment is due [date]. Questions? Call 940-323-4303. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.
